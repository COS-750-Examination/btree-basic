A **binary search tree** (BST) keeps one key per node:

- everything in the **left** subtree is **smaller** than the node;
- everything in the **right** subtree is **larger**.

The **root** is the top node, a **leaf** has no children, and the **height** is the number of levels.

The ordering rule holds across the **whole subtree**, not just between a parent and its children. Always compare at every node on the way down.
