## The key idea

Searching follows **one path from the root down**. In each node:

1. Compare the key with the node's keys, from left to right.
2. **Found it?** Stop.
3. Otherwise go down the child **before the first larger key**, or the **last child** if the key is larger than all of them.
4. Reach a leaf without finding it? The key **isn't in the tree**.

## Worked example

Order 3. Root `[20 | 40]` with children `[5 | 12]`, `[25 | 30]` and `[50 | 60]`.

**Search for 30:**

1. Root: 30 is larger than 20 and smaller than 40, so take the **middle child**.
2. `[25 | 30]`: found **30**. Two nodes visited.

**Search for 45:**

1. Root: 45 is larger than 40, so take the **last child**.
2. `[50 | 60]`: 45 is smaller than 50, but this is a leaf, so **45 is not in the tree**.

## Watch out

- **Never search sideways.** Once you leave a node you never come back to it or its neighbours.
- **The number of nodes visited equals the levels walked,** never more than the tree's height.

## Check yourself

In the same tree, which nodes does a search for **12** visit, and is it found?

## Answer

The root `[20 | 40]` (12 is smaller than 20, so take the **first child**), then `[5 | 12]`, where **12 is found**. Two nodes.
